UnifyWeb is open source under the [MIT License](LICENSE). It is provided **"as is", without warranty of any kind**, express or implied, including fitness for a particular purpose. You use it at your own risk.

UnifyWeb sends commands directly to your receiver and devices. Pairing and unpairing, changing settings, remapping buttons, running rules and writing onboard profiles all change how your hardware behaves. A mistake or an unexpected device response could, for example:

- leave a device unpaired so it has to be paired again;
- change buttons, sensitivity or lighting in ways you did not intend;
- require a device's onboard profiles to be reset to factory settings.

Keep a wired or spare keyboard and mouse to hand when pairing or changing input devices.

## How it was built

UnifyWeb was written from publicly available information about Logitech's HID++ protocol and tested against a simulated receiver. Some features, including Bolt pairing, lighting effects and writing onboard profiles, have not been verified on every real device.

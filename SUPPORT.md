# Support

UnifyWeb is a free, unpaid project. **No support is offered.**

- There is no help desk, email address or response-time commitment.
- Questions, bug reports and feature requests may be read when time allows, but may get no reply and may not be acted on.
- There is no guarantee that UnifyWeb will keep working with future browsers, operating systems or Logitech devices.

## Help yourself first

- The Help page in the app covers setup and the most common problems.
- The protocol log on the Help page shows every message exchanged with your receiver, which usually shows what went wrong.
- The README in the repository explains how the app works and how to build it.

## Problems with your Logitech hardware

For anything about the hardware itself, including faults, warranty claims or replacement receivers, contact Logitech. UnifyWeb is not a Logitech product and Logitech will not support it.

## Security issues

Security problems can still be reported privately, as described in the [security policy](SECURITY.md). Reports are welcome, but there is no guaranteed response time.
